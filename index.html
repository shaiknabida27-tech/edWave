Math Arena

    Multiply by 11

    For a two-digit number, put the sum of the digits in the middle. 23 × 11: 2, then 2+3=5, then 3 gives 253. If the sum is 10 or more, carry 1: 78 × 11 gives 7, 15, 8 which becomes 858.

    Square a number ending in 5

    Multiply the first digit by the next digit up, then write 25. 35²: 3 × 4 = 12, so 1225. 85²: 8 × 9 = 72, so 7225. Great for Square Summit.

    Multiply by 5

    Multiply by 10, then halve it. 46 × 5: 460 ÷ 2 = 230.

    Find 10%, 5% and 20%

    10% means move the decimal one place left. 5% is half of that, and 20% is double it. For 360: 10% is 36, 5% is 18, 20% is 72.
